# Assumptions

## Random Assignment

Users are randomly assigned to control and treatment groups.

---

## Stable Pricing

Subscription prices remain unchanged throughout the experiment.

---

## Healthy Infrastructure

Stripe API maintains a 98.5% success rate.

---

## Correct Event Logging

The following events are assumed to be captured correctly:

- sign_up
- complete_onboarding
- create_graphic
- click_export
- view_pricing
- purchase_subscribed

---

## External Effects Negligible

No major:

- seasonality
- holidays
- promotions
- marketing campaigns

affected the results.

---

## Goal of Experiment

Maximize premium subscriptions rather than maximizing engagement.
